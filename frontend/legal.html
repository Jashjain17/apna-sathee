10. DATA BREACH NOTIFICATION PROCEDURE

A personal data breach includes any accidental or unlawful access to, disclosure of, alteration of, or destruction of user data — including accidental sharing of a Google Sheet, unauthorised access to a team Google account, sending user data to the wrong person, or a mentor accessing data they should not have.

In the event of a confirmed or suspected personal data breach, we shall adhere to the following procedure:

  • Step 1 — Contain: Immediately restrict access to the affected data, revoke any unauthorised access permissions, and where technically feasible, recover or permanently delete the compromised data.
  • Step 2 — Notify Regulator: Notify the Data Protection Board of India within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach, as required under the DPDP Act 2023.
  • Step 3 — Notify Users: Notify all affected users within 48 hours of confirming the breach via email to their registered email address, clearly describing what happened, what data was affected, and what steps we are taking.
  • Step 4 — Investigate: Conduct an internal investigation and document findings in our internal breach register for regulatory compliance.
  • Step 5 — Disclose: Communicate breach details and remediation steps directly to affected users via email. A summary disclosure will be published on the Platform as soon as practically possible.

The founding team member designated as Data Protection Officer (dpo@apnasaathi.in) is responsible for leading all breach response and notification activities. We maintain an internal breach register documenting all suspected or confirmed data breaches, the nature of data affected, steps taken, and notifications sent. This register is available for inspection by the Data Protection Board upon request.
